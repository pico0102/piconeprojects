Maui, Mom & Me

Eight days, seven nights, built around one big day (Hana), two ocean days, and real time to just sit on the lānai. Nothing back-to-back, no luau, and nothing that's an hours-long trek.

Easy day Moderate Full day out

Easy
Tue, Sept 15 · land 4:35pm

You land mid-afternoon — this is a half-day by the time you get to the hotel. Check in, get sand under your feet, and don't plan anything that requires being anywhere on time.

Aft.
Check in, then the Beach WalkA paved, mostly flat oceanfront path running the length of Kaanapali, right past the hotel — good for stretching your legs without committing to anything.
Eve.
Whalers Village for an easy dinner, then watch the cliff-dive torch-lighting ceremony from the beach at sunset (free, nightly, no ticket)
02Recovery Day, Black Rock
Easy
Wed, Sept 16

A buffer day between the flight and Hana — you just did roughly 15 hours of travel, no reason to follow it straight into a 10–12 hour driving day. Deliberately nothing planned.

AM
Pool or beach, no agenda. Sleep in, shake off the jet lag.
Late AM
Pu'u Keka'a (Black Rock), a 10-minute walk up the Beach Walk from the Hilton — some of the best shore snorkeling on this coast, easy sandy entry, usually calm and full of turtles and fish. No boat needed.
PM
Nap, lānai, room service. Seriously — leave this open. Also a good evening to download the Shaka Guide tour over the hotel wifi, ready for tomorrow.
03Road to Hana
Full day
Thu, Sept 17

A weekday, with a full day already behind you to recover from the flight — no reservation, no deadline, no back-to-back with travel day.

Shaka Guide fits right in: the Classic Road to Hana tour already covers Ho'okipa, Ke'anae, 'Ohe'o Gulch/Pipiwai, and Hamoa Beach, and returns via this same Hana Highway rather than the back road — no changes needed to the plan below. Download it over the hotel wifi the night before so it's ready offline — cell service disappears for long stretches past Ke'anae.
Mile 9
Ho'okipa Lookout — paved lot right above the beach, sea turtles hauled out on the sand most mornings. Zero walking.
Mile 16
Ke'anae Peninsula — flat half-mile loop past the old church and lava coastline; stop as much or as little as feels good.
Hana
Lunch in Hana town — Hana Ranch Restaurant (open daily, 11am–9pm) is the reliable option; Poke Bob's and Hana Farms food trucks are also open most days.
Past Hana
'Ohe'o Gulch (Seven Sacred Pools) — view the pools from the boardwalk overlook. If you've got energy left, the first stretch of the Pipiwai Trail to Makahiku Falls overlook is about 20–30 minutes round trip on a real path and ends at a genuinely great waterfall view.This is Haleakalā National Park's Kīpahulu district — a $30/vehicle NPS entrance fee applies. Buy online at recreation.gov beforehand, or pay by card at the entrance station — no cash accepted on-site.
Koki Beach
Red sand, sea arch, views of 'Alau Island — no reservation, just pull off and walk down. A few minutes past Hana town.
Hamoa Beach
Consistently ranked one of Maui's best beaches — turquoise water, no reservation needed. Good spot to get in the water and unwind before the drive back.
Return
Drive back the way you came (Hana Highway), not the Kahekili "back road" — it's unpaved in stretches and most rental agreements exclude it anyway.
Reality check: this is a 10–12 hour day with 600+ curves. Pack snacks and motion-sickness remedies, fill the tank in Kahului before you go (no gas past Paia), and don't feel obligated to hit every stop — nothing here has a deadline, so trim as the day goes.
04Snorkel Sail
Moderate
Fri, Sept 18

The one "must-do" boat trip — someone else does the work today. The Kaanapali options win on convenience — no early drive, just a short walk from the hotel.

Top pick
Teralani Premier Snorkel Sail — departs the Aqualani kiosk at the Outrigger Kaanapali, an easy walk up the Beach Walk from the Hilton. $179.95/adult, includes breakfast, BBQ lunch, and two drinks each on the return sail. Book direct at teralani.com →
Close second
Trilogy Discover Ka'anapali — departs the Sheraton's beach lawn, also an easy walk. $214/adult, similar format, Trilogy's reputation is excellent. Book direct at sailtrilogy.com →
If you'd rather use the discount
Pride of Maui, Sail Maui, or Maui Classic Charters all run from Ma'alaea Harbor (~45 min drive) and are bookable via Costco Travel → Viator for 15% off. Similar trip, more travel time.
PM
These trips usually return by early afternoon — rest of the day is open for the pool.

06Kapalua Bay & Lahaina
Moderate
Sun, Sept 20

A calm morning swim, then an afternoon in a town that's genuinely worth the visit — Front Street reopened to the public August 1, 2026, after the wildfire. Dinner reservation locked in for the evening.

AM
Kapalua Bay — a sheltered, crescent-shaped bay about 20 min north, widely considered the calmest, easiest snorkeling on this coast. Gentle sandy entry, protected from surf and wind most mornings.
PM
Lahaina — the rebuilt stretch of Front Street: shops and the harbor. Note the historic core and the Banyan Tree courtyard are still an active rebuild site and roped off — plan to observe respectfully. Keep this loose enough to be at the restaurant by 5:30.
5:30pm
Dinner at Mala Ocean Tavern — reservation confirmed. Oceanfront, and sunset on Sept 20 is around 6:25pm, so you're likely to catch it right from the table.

Skipped on purpose

Cut for a reason, not by accident.

Wai'ānapanapa State Park—Its timed-entry reservation only had a 10:00–12:30pm slot with a 10:30am parking cutoff — real drive time from Kaanapali runs ~3h40m nonstop, leaving almost no margin. Swapped for Koki and Hamoa Beach, both reservation-free.
Luau (any of them)—You said no — noted and left out entirely.
Whale watching—Humpback season runs roughly December–April; September is well outside it.
Haleakalā sunrise—3am wake-up, timed reservation, 40°F summit, two-hour drive each way — the opposite of the pace you want. Skip it guilt-free.
Pipiwai Trail to Waimoku Falls—The full trail is a genuine 4-mile round trip — too long to bolt onto an already-full Hana day. The short first stretch to Makahiku Falls is included on Day 3 instead.
