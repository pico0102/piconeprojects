Book before you go

  • Flights OGG both ways
  • Rental car
  • Hotel (7 nights Kaanapali)
  • Snorkel sail — Molokini
  • Haleakalā sunrise permit (recreation.gov)
  • Wai'anapanapa reservation (Hawaii DLNR)
  • Mama's Fish House (or Star Noodle)
  • Merriman's Kapalua
  • Download Gypsy Guide (Hana)
  • Snorkel gear rental or purchase
  • SPF 50+ (reef safe)
  • Warm layer for Haleakalā

Left on the table

Things we looked at and decided against for this trip.

  • Road to Hana overnight stay adds a full day of driving to get back; not worth it for 7 nights
  • Helicopter tour expensive and depends on weather; better to put money into the snorkel sail
  • Surfing lessons September conditions in Kaanapali can be flat — not the best window
  • Iao Valley State Park beautiful but the hike is short and near Wailuku — out of the way for a Kaanapali base
  • Whale watching peak season is December–April; September you might see them on the snorkel sail, but don't book a dedicated tour
